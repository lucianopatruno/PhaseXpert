# Advanced multicomponent phase-production audit

## Objective and decision

This 2026-08-28 milestone audited the existing research-only multicomponent bubble, dew, tangent-plane-distance (TPD), TP-flash, phase-classification, and pseudo-arclength envelope stack against independent dry CO2-rich evidence. The result is **no production promotion**. The numerical infrastructure is valuable research machinery, but neither its accepted-row coverage nor the available composition geometry supports a bounded production domain for the requested ternary, quaternary, or five-component systems. Unknown and structured failure therefore remain first-class outcomes.

The model remains teqp v0.23.1 commit `a68eb9cabf47af2c4aba0d272ac10fbca4c10eca`. Existing identity artifacts remain valid for component ordering, pure-fluid identities, reducing/departure functions, composition mapping, molar masses, SI pressure/temperature/molar-density units, chemical-potential convention, and generic N-component model construction. No coefficient or binary interaction parameter was fitted or changed.

## Evidence inventory

Ottøy et al. 2020, DOI `10.1016/j.fluid.2019.112444`, remains the only complete primary CO2/N2/CH4 row set: 31 liquid-specified bubble and 31 vapor-specified dew rows, with measured opposite-phase compositions. The CC BY 4.0 source artifact preserves published four-decimal fractions; where rounding leaves a 0.0001 closure difference, CO2 is explicitly calculated as `1-xN2-xCH4`, recorded per row, and never proportionally normalized.

Ke et al. 2017, DOI `10.1016/j.ijggc.2016.11.003`, supplies 63 exact synthetic CO2/Ar/N2 boundary rows from open thesis tables: 24 bubble and 39 dew. Pressure expanded uncertainty is 0.04 MPa. The experiment identifies the boundary of a known overall mixture but does not measure incipient-phase composition or phase fraction. Theveneau et al. 2020, DOI `10.1021/acs.jced.9b01082`, supplies 31 CO2/CH4/H2S interior rows for the independent flash check. Tenorio 2015 CO2/N2/H2 was re-reviewed as relevant synthetic boundary context but does not validate the requested four/five-component systems. Razmjoo 2026 supplies homogeneous density only and is used solely for robustness diagnostics.

Searches through 2026-08-28 covered NIST ThermoML/TRC, journal and supplementary pages, institutional repositories, author records, EOS-CG/CCS literature, and newer citations. The 2025 CO2/CH4/N2 Gibbs-ensemble work is molecular simulation, not independent experimental validation. No new lawful exact experimental boundary set was found for CO2/N2/O2/Ar, CO2/N2/CH4/H2, or CO2/N2/Ar/CH4/H2. No PDF, paywalled table, figure digitization, or secondary software output is committed as validation truth.

## Native audit and acceptance rules

The call chain is `NativeTeqpEngine` actor -> bounded C ABI -> local C++ model/solver -> teqp Helmholtz primitives. Bubble/dew use `px_teqp_calculate_ncomponent_vle` and `solve_ncomponent_vle`; TPD uses `px_teqp_calculate_ncomponent_tpd` and `solve_tpd`; flash uses `px_teqp_calculate_ncomponent_tp_flash` and `solve_tp_flash`; envelope tracing uses `px_teqp_trace_ncomponent_phase_envelope` and its pseudo-arclength corrector. C entry points reject null pointers, duplicate IDs, nonpositive fractions, nonfinite states, and sums outside 1 by more than `1e-10`; no normalization or EOS fallback occurs. `NativeTeqpEngine` serializes Swift entry, and native calls construct local model/solver state without a shared continuation cache.

Bubble/dew variables are log densities and softmax/log-ratio incipient compositions. Deterministic multistart Levenberg-Marquardt uses a central finite-difference Jacobian, damping, and backtracking. Acceptance requires positive finite pressure, distinct phases, liquid density above vapor density, maximum dimensionless chemical-potential residual below `2e-7`, relative pressure residual below `2e-7`, and positive total-Helmholtz Hessian eigenvalues for both phases. The failure review found 17 Ottøy stationary pairs rejected for local instability; V28 additionally reached negative pressure/severe instability. These are not missing brackets, Jacobian failures, tolerance failures, or invalid composition closure, so loosening tolerances or adding silent retries would be scientifically wrong.

TPD enumerates stable density roots, selects the minimum-Gibbs reference, and searches deterministic feed, component-enriched, and pair-enriched simplex starts. It reports total/successful starts, iterations, root evaluations, and distinct minima. TPD below `-2e-6` is unstable, `-2e-6...-1e-9` is near-neutral, and at least `-1e-9` is stable. A failed global search is never relabelled stable. Flash additionally requires material balance below `2e-8`, chemical-potential and both pressure residuals below `2e-7`, density ordering, distinct compositions, positive local stability, and independent TPD postchecks on both returned phases.

Pseudo-arclength continuation starts only from an accepted boundary state, uses a scaled predictor/corrector with adaptive steps, replaces the initial temperature tangent with a secant tangent, rejects composition/pressure jumps, counts turning points, and distinguishes temperature-limit, capacity, step-underflow, branch-continuity, initial-failure, and critical-like termination. Composition and density coalescence remain critical-like diagnostics, not an asserted critical point. No algorithm change was made in this milestone because the observed promotion blockers are evidence coverage and thermodynamic/stability rejection rather than an unaddressed safe numerical defect.

## Validation results

Ottøy bubble accepted 21/31 rows. Pressure MAE is 0.038069 MPa, AARD 0.542199%, bias -0.446304%, RMS 0.649702%, worst absolute error 0.111137 MPa, and worst relative error -1.268690%. Opposite vapor-composition MAE for CO2/N2/CH4 is 0.003419/0.002377/0.001830 mole fraction; RMS is 0.004287/0.002976/0.002542. Ottøy dew accepted 23/31 rows. Pressure MAE is 0.119443 MPa, AARD 1.504217%, bias -1.177069%, RMS 2.620868%, worst absolute error 0.956776 MPa, and worst relative error -10.279512%. Opposite liquid-composition MAE is 0.005410/0.003053/0.002523; RMS is 0.010531/0.006048/0.004609. Experimental pressure uncertainty is not encoded row-by-row, so no fabricated within-uncertainty fraction is reported.

Ke accepted 40/63 rows: pressure MAE 0.050296 MPa, AARD 0.708109%, bias -0.316260%, RMS 0.953432%, worst -2.701952%. M1 bubble accepted 8/11, AARD 0.968047%; M1 dew 15/20, 0.595033%; M2 bubble 7/13, 0.176874%; M2 dew 10/19, 1.041637%. Every branch contains failures, aggregate MAE exceeds the reported 0.04 MPa expanded uncertainty, and no opposite-phase composition was measured. Theveneau flash accepted 0/31 rows after all hardened postchecks; phase-composition and fraction errors are therefore unavailable rather than silently calculated from rejected states.

Representative simulator timings recorded by the 2026-08-28 native rerun are: Ottøy boundary point median/mean/worst 470.7/458.7/1283.6 ms over 62 calls; Ke point 241.2/297.1/1312.5 ms over 63; and Theveneau validation flash median/mean/worst 4072.8/3853.8/7494.5 ms over 31 calls. Existing reproducible benchmarks additionally record ternary TPD at 1632.7 ms, an accepted self-consistency flash demonstrator at 4409.4 ms, and an 11-point research envelope at 559.3 ms median, about 50.8 ms per accepted point. These paths remain asynchronous research diagnostics and are not introduced into synchronous production UI.

## System decisions and product impact

CO2/N2/CH4: **KEEP RESEARCH-ONLY / NO PROMOTION** for bubble, dew, classification, flash, and envelope. CO2/Ar/N2: **KEEP RESEARCH-ONLY / NO PROMOTION** because only 40/63 boundary rows pass. CO2/N2/O2/Ar, CO2/N2/CH4/H2, and CO2/N2/Ar/CH4/H2: **NO PROMOTION** because no independent exact VLE boundary or interior data validate these component sets. CO2/CH4/H2S: **KEEP RESEARCH-ONLY / NO PROMOTION** because 0/31 flash rows pass. CO2/N2 remains a binary control and receives no new multicomponent capability. Secondary CO/H2S phase behavior remains research-only.

There are no promoted domains, capability-matrix changes, provider-routing changes, phase-classification changes, phase-envelope changes, Phase Diagram changes, sweep changes, or UI changes. Existing Advanced density, O2 acoustic, H2S density, pure CO2, General Properties, water-equilibrium, dry-mixture safety, Saved Case, sweep, and built-in behavior remain unchanged. Northern Lights, Brevik, Porthos, and Aramis do not enter a validated Advanced multicomponent phase domain.
