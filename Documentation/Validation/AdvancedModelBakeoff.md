# Advanced CCS model bake-off

## Objective and baseline

This 2026-08-28 audit asks whether an identity-complete, published, untuned and iOS-feasible alternative can support a materially broader independently validated CO2-rich CCS domain than PhaseXpert's current EOS-CG-2021/custom multifluid implementation. The unchanged baseline is teqp 0.23.1 commit `a68eb9cabf47af2c4aba0d272ac10fbca4c10eca`. Its static component, reducing/departure-function and ordering identity remains established by `EOSCGStaticIdentityAudit.json` and `EOSCG2021TargetModelData.json`; the density and phase results from PRs #76 and #77 were reused because neither model nor evidence rows changed. No production provider was modified during candidate assessment.

## Candidate search and identity

Primary publications, Crossref, OpenAlex, publisher metadata, article supplements, existing lawful repository artifacts, teqp factories/model data and the pinned CoolProp database were searched through 2026-08-28. The serious candidates were GERG-2008 (Kunz and Wagner, DOI `10.1021/je300655b`), EOS-LNG (Thol et al., DOI `10.1063/1.5093800`), the open-access ZMS CO2/CH4/N2 Helmholtz EOS (Xu, Mao and Zheng, DOI `10.3390/app13063659`), the Xu-Mao-Zheng CO2/N2/O2/Ar general Helmholtz EOS (DOI `10.1016/j.fluid.2022.113703`), the newly published Xu-Mao H2/CH4/C2H6/C3H8/CO2/N2 Helmholtz EOS (DOI `10.1016/j.fluid.2026.114851`), CoolProp 8.0.0 HEOS, UMR-PRU and published PC-SAFT/CPA/SAFT-VR Mie families. The machine-readable artifact records each formulation's component scope, coefficient source, implementation status, teqp/iOS implications, independence and decision.

GERG-2008 is not a materially distinct alternative for the key CO2/N2, CO2/CH4 and CO2/H2S pairs because the PhaseXpert baseline already inherits those GERG records; it also omits O2 and Ar. EOS-LNG is a methane-rich LNG formulation with insufficient target-component and CO2-rich validation coverage. ZMS is the strongest ternary lead: its abstract reports 0.40% ternary PVTx AARD and the article is CC BY 4.0, but the referenced complete Fortran/model package was not directly retrievable, exact term identity was not established, and its training-data overlap must be separated from PhaseXpert holdouts. The 2023 CO2/N2/O2/Ar and 2026 H2-containing Xu-Mao formulations are directly relevant but closed-access coefficient tables/model packages were unavailable through legitimate routes; no coefficients were inferred. CoolProp HEOS is already implemented in the separate General provider and was evaluated only against experiments, never used as truth. UMR-PRU's favorable Petropoulou result is fitted to those same isotherms and is circular here. PC-SAFT, CPA and SAFT-VR Mie applications require model-specific fitted binary/group parameters; no complete untuned target matrix was found, and zeroing or assembling absent interactions would violate the milestone rules.

## Common evidence and quantitative comparison

The baseline evidence index comprises 64 Mazzoccoli CO2/N2 rows, 180 Ghafri and 271 Liu CO2/CH4 rows, 19 Souissi plus 27 Owuna CO2/H2 rows, 330 Razmjoo dry-multicomponent density rows, 62 Ottøy ternary VLE rows, 63 Ke/George boundary rows and 31 Theveneau interior VLE rows. Existing O2, Ar and H2S artifacts were included where the candidate representation supported them. Mantovani and Brugge remain evidence leads without newly lawful exact row encodings; they were not converted from figures.

EOS-CG baseline full-map density statistics are: Mazzoccoli CO2/N2 64/64 converged, 30.2987% AARD, -29.7972% bias, 39.6722% RMS and -87.0675% worst, with only five production rows; Ghafri CO2/CH4 180/180, 1.39822% AARD, 1.39822% bias, 1.68907% RMS and 5.15214% worst; Liu 271/271, 0.783879% AARD and 7.67509% worst; Souissi CO2/H2 19/19, 0.096971% AARD, 0.093072% bias, 0.148968% RMS and 0.408391% worst; Razmjoo 171/330 converged, 0.610303% AARD, -0.108497% bias, 0.844124% RMS and 4.37279% worst. Baseline VLE remains Ottøy bubble 21/31 accepted, 0.038069 MPa MAE, 0.542199% AARD and -1.26869% worst; dew 23/31 accepted, 0.119443 MPa MAE, 1.504217% AARD and -10.2795% worst; Ke 40/63 accepted, 0.050296 MPa MAE, 0.708109% AARD and -2.70195% worst; Theveneau 0/31 accepted.

The only identity-complete alternative already executable across several common datasets, CoolProp 8.0.0 HEOS, scored: CO2/N2 64/64, 22.4225% AARD, 130.113% RMS and 1032.55% worst; Ghafri CO2/CH4 180/180, 1.40001% AARD, 1.69092% RMS and 5.15563% worst; Souissi CO2/H2 19/19, 0.097349% AARD, 0.149409% RMS and 0.409137% worst; Lozano CO2/O2 92/92, 4.67476% AARD, 43.7634% RMS and 419.759% worst; Mazzoccoli CO2/Ar 122/122, 4.10415% AARD, 11.6830% RMS and -51.4195% worst. Good narrow slices already belong to General Properties; catastrophic local density/root selections and absent common VLE evidence make this unsuitable as a broader Advanced formulation. Candidates lacking exact implementations have no fabricated numerical metrics or runtime.

## Stability, performance and iOS feasibility

The EOS-CG stability record remains 44/62 accepted Ottøy boundary points, 40/63 Ke points and 0/31 Theveneau hardened flashes. Existing representative simulator performance is Ottøy median/worst 470.7/1283.6 ms, Ke 241.2/1312.5 ms, TPD 1632.7 ms and accepted demonstrator flash 4409.4 ms. CoolProp is already shipped and adds no binary size, model-construction cache or ABI cost. Other candidates cannot be honestly timed without exact implementations. GERG and generalized Helmholtz candidates are structurally compatible with local derivative/root infrastructure, but GERG factory code is excluded from the present teqp iOS build; the SAFT/CPA factories are likewise excluded and would materially enlarge native scope. No candidate supplied enough scientific advantage to justify that rebuild.

## Decision

GERG-2008, EOS-LNG, CoolProp HEOS, UMR-PRU and the combined PC-SAFT/CPA/SAFT-VR Mie family are `REJECT`. ZMS CO2/CH4/N2, Xu-Mao-Zheng CO2/N2/O2/Ar and Xu-Mao 2026 H2/CH4/CO2/N2 are `PROMISING — NEEDS MORE DATA`; they require lawful exact model packages, parameter/training identity audits, independent holdouts and deterministic density/VLE/stability benchmarks before code or production consideration. No candidate materially beat EOS-CG over a broader connected independent domain, no capability was promoted, no multi-model routing was implemented, and all existing exact/discrete Advanced gates remain unchanged. Northern Lights, Brevik, Porthos and Aramis therefore have no new Advanced support.

## Reproducibility and integrity

Run `python3 Scripts/validate-advanced-model-bakeoff.py`, `swift test --package-path PhaseXpertCore --filter AdvancedModelBakeoffDecisionTests`, `swift test --package-path PhaseXpertCore --filter AdvancedCCSCapabilityMatrixTests`, and `swift test --package-path PhaseXpertCore`. The validator cross-checks copied metrics against the PR #76, PR #77 and CoolProp experimental artifacts rather than creating duplicate truth rows. No coefficients or BIPs were invented, fitted, tuned or substituted; no validation points were invented; no digitized figure was promoted; no software result was treated as experimental evidence; no invalid composition was normalized; no unsupported component was dropped; no unstable state was accepted; and negative results were retained.
