# Advanced CCS multicomponent phase behavior — 2026-08-24

**Decision: no new production capability.** The new generic continuation, strengthened TPD diagnostics, and TP-flash postchecks remain research-only. The independent boundary campaign converged on only 40/63 rows, and the independent interior campaign converged on 0/31 rows. No capability-matrix, provider-routing, built-in, or production UI change is scientifically justified.

## Architecture audit

The existing implementation was retained rather than replaced. `Native/TeqpBridge/src/PhaseXpertTeqpBridge.cpp` already supplied arbitrary-N EOS-CG pressure, chemical potentials, total Helmholtz Hessian/local stability, logarithmic density-root enumeration, deterministic multistart TPD, generic bubble/dew equations, and a direct TP-flash formulation. `PhaseXpertTeqpBridge.h` provided bounded C buffers and structured equilibrium/stability statuses. `NativeTeqpEngine.swift` and `TeqpProvider.swift` provided async Swift isolation and research diagnostics; production phase-envelope routing remained limited to previously accepted pure CO2 and exact CO2+CH4 gates. Existing Ottøy tests and artifacts already covered 62 ternary boundary specifications. Limitations were fixed-temperature boundary multistart without generic continuation, incomplete TPD start diagnostics, and TP flash reporting its pre-flash feed TPD as a postcheck.

## Solver work

The new arbitrary-N envelope ABI starts from an actually converged bubble or dew state, uses the existing equilibrium equations, and applies a scaled pseudo-arclength predictor/corrector. A secant tangent replaces the initial temperature tangent after the first accepted step, so temperature turning points can be followed and counted. The corrector uses a bounded damped least-squares Newton iteration. Step size grows or shrinks with corrector effort, failures halve the step, attempts and capacity are bounded, valid preceding points are retained, and termination distinguishes temperature limit, capacity, step underflow, branch-continuity rejection, initial failure, and a critical-like endpoint. Composition and pressure jumps reject branch hopping. Phase-composition and relative-density coalescence are recorded only as critical-like diagnostics, never as a validated critical prediction.

TPD remains deterministic and now adds deterministic pair-enriched starts to the feed and single-component-enriched starts. Its ABI reports total and successful starts as well as iterations, density-root evaluations, and distinct minima, so a failed search is not silently labelled stable. TP flash retains the direct chemical-potential/material-balance formulation. A candidate split must still have finite positive densities, normalized positive compositions, bounded beta, small material/pressure/chemical-potential residuals, density ordering, phase distinction, and positive local Hessian eigenvalues; it now also runs independent global TPD postchecks on both returned phases and rejects failed or unstable postchecks.

The focused deterministic envelope test accepted more than four points twice with byte-identical temperatures and pressures, normalized both phases at every point, positive ordered densities, bounded attempts, and explicit rejection of an unclosed feed. The established ternary flash demonstrator verifies component material balance below 2e-8, log-fugacity equality below 2e-7, both pressure residuals below 2e-7, normalized phases, beta in (0,1), positive phase stability, and deterministic repeatability. These are solver-consistency checks, not accuracy evidence.

## Primary-data acquisition audit

The search covered NIST ThermoML/TRC, journal and supplementary pages, institutional/accepted manuscripts, theses, Crossref/DOI relations, CCS/EOS-CG/GERG literature, and backward references. Sources inspected beyond the previously accepted Ottøy dataset included:

- Ke et al., *International Journal of Greenhouse Gas Control* 56 (2017) 55-66, DOI `10.1016/j.ijggc.2016.11.003`: exact open thesis tables for two CO2+Ar+N2 phase envelopes (63 averaged boundary rows) and a third CO2+Ar+H2 envelope. The two priority CO2+Ar+N2 families were transcribed exactly; published Ar/N2 values are retained and CO2 is explicitly closed as `1-xAr-xN2`. Synthetic boundary detection means no opposite-phase x/y or beta was measured.
- Theveneau et al., *Journal of Chemical & Engineering Data* 65 (2020) 1802-1813, DOI `10.1021/acs.jced.9b01082`: the HAL accepted manuscript provides 31 exact interior CH4+CO2+H2S states with certified/analyzed global composition and measured liquid and vapor compositions. These rows are a valid predictive TP-flash challenge; phase fraction was not directly measured but is potentially reconstructible by the lever rule if x/y predictions first pass.
- Tenorio et al., *International Journal of Greenhouse Gas Control* 41 (2015) 68-81, DOI `10.1016/j.ijggc.2015.06.009`: two CO2+N2+H2 phase envelopes and four binaries were inspected. It is a boundary-only synthetic campaign with global mixture compositions, not an interior x/y/phase-fraction source; no second transcription was made after the exact Ke boundary campaign and Theveneau interior campaign were secured.
- Ottøy et al., DOI `10.1016/j.fluid.2019.112444`: retained only for the required regression; it was not retranscribed or re-gated.
- Gernert's CCS-mixture dissertation and the primary CO2+N2+O2 literature it traces were inspected. The available ternary material did not yield a new lawful exact row set with independently measured z, x, y, and beta suitable for this gate.
- Sanchez-Vicente and Trusler, *Energies* 15 (2022) 3936, DOI `10.3390/en15113936`, reports analytical CO2+H2O+N2 phase compositions, but the aqueous/wet system is outside this dry multicomponent milestone and existing water validation was explicitly excluded.
- Theveneau's CH4+CO2+H2S rows were preferred over hydrate-bearing CH4+CO2+H2O papers and older hydrocarbon-rich ternaries because they provide exact z/T/P/x/y without graph digitization.

Downloaded papers remained temporary and are not committed. The committed JSON records provenance, method, pressure basis, uncertainties, composition basis, measured observables, and the absence of measured phase fraction.

## Independent results

`KeGeorge2017CO2ArN2PhaseBoundary.json` contains 63 exact boundary rows: 24 bubble and 39 dew. Forty converged (63.49%). Across converged rows, pressure MAE was 0.05030 MPa, AARD 0.7081%, signed bias -0.3163%, RMS 0.9534%, and worst relative deviation -2.7020%. M1 bubble was 8/11, AARD 0.9680%; M1 dew 15/20, 0.5950%; M2 bubble 7/13, 0.1769%; and M2 dew 10/19, 1.0416%. Median/mean/worst point runtime was 244.1/325.4/1331.1 ms. Aggregate MAE exceeds the reported 0.04 MPa expanded pressure uncertainty and failures interrupt every branch. No phase-composition metric exists because the synthetic boundary experiment did not measure the incipient phase composition. Bubble pressure, dew pressure, and envelope therefore fail promotion.

`Theveneau2020TernaryInteriorVLE.json` contains 31 independent interior rows with z/T/P and both measured phases. The hardened predictive TP flash converged on 0/31; median/mean/worst runtime was 4303.0/4060.2/7480.0 ms. Accordingly there are no defensible liquid/vapor composition or beta error metrics. Although experimental beta can in principle be reconstructed from the complete measured lever rule, beta validation cannot proceed when the predictive flash produces no accepted split. TP-flash x/y, beta, and phase classification remain research-only.

Representative focused timings on the iPhone 17 simulator were: boundary point median/mean/worst 244.1/325.4/1331.1 ms over 63 rows; an 11-point pseudo-arclength envelope 559.3/559.3/570.3 ms over two deterministic traces (50.8 ms per accepted point); TPD 1632.7 ms for one seven-start ternary case; and the accepted TP-flash consistency demonstrator 4409.4 ms for one case. The experimental 31-row flash sweep is the broader timing sample above. These research paths are asynchronous but are not suitable for synchronous UI work.

Ke et al. experimentally bracket near-critical/maxcondentherm behavior, but no independent comparison of a predicted mixture-critical point passed. The engine terminates on numerical phase coalescence as `critical-like` only. No critical-point production claim is made.

## Product decision

No property passed a capability-specific production gate. `AdvancedCCSCapabilityMatrix`, providers, validation presentation, shields, built-ins, Phase Envelope UI, Flash UI, and pressure/temperature margins are intentionally unchanged. Existing built-ins were not altered and no new gate can apply to them. The research ABI is asynchronous at the Swift boundary, but observed envelope/boundary and especially TP-flash runtimes confirm it must not be placed synchronously on the UI thread. No production-gate enumeration was added because there is no new production branch to enumerate.

Machine-readable source and decision artifacts accompany this report. CoolProp was not used as validation truth, no BIPs or data were fitted or invented, no experimental plot was digitized, and accepted density/acoustic/water campaigns were not repeated except for the prescribed focused final regressions.
